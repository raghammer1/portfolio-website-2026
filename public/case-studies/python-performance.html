Faster data processing with Python and Polars

I replaced a costly legacy processing approach with Python and Polars, achieving an approximately 90× speed improvement. This is a high-level account of my contribution; employer code, data and internal architecture remain private.

Problem and role

In my Associate Software Engineer role, I worked on a long-running data-processing workflow in retail credit decisioning. My contribution was the Python/Polars implementation that improved its execution time. The earlier approach used SAS/pandas.

Constraints

The workflow sat in a banking context, so a useful optimisation needed to preserve the meaning of the processing. Confidential business rules and datasets cannot be reproduced here. No customer information, internal identifiers or screenshots are included.

Approach and engineering decision

The implemented change moved the processing approach to Polars within Python. This is a change to data execution rather than a claim about larger infrastructure. I can discuss the role of a dataframe engine and rule readability publicly; specific queries, data shapes, profiling results and migration details are not published.

Alternatives and trade offs

The known baseline is the legacy SAS/pandas approach. The available public evidence does not establish a formal comparison with every alternative, or which internal operation dominated runtime. A fair technical discussion should separate the measured improvement from general reasons one might choose Polars, such as its execution model. I do not present those general reasons as a reconstructed account of my implementation.

Correctness and validation

The performance result is my reported professional result, confirmed in October 2026. A shareable benchmark dataset, hardware specification, timing distribution and regression-test record are not available here. The appropriate follow-up discussion is how equivalent inputs and outputs were checked, which workload was measured and how the change was reviewed, without disclosing employer material.

Result

Approximately 90× faster processing and Quarterly MVP recognition. I use this single result consistently rather than pairing it with incompatible hours-to-seconds figures. Related work includes bidirectional translation between Polars rules and plain-English pseudocode.

Limitations and next improvement

This account demonstrates an engineering contribution, not a reproducible public benchmark or a universal claim that Polars is 90× faster.
